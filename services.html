Our legacy
is deeply rooted in Indonesia's coffee heritage

Established in 1991, we work hand in hand with farmers in Toraja, South Sulawesi – and other rich coffee regions like Gayo, Mandailing, Java, Bali, Flores and Papua. Every cup we serve tells a story of our farmers, our culture, and the artistry of brewing perfected over three decades

Beyond coffee, we are a place where friends meet, ideas spark, and memories are made.

Guided by tradition yet inspired by innovation


we continue to craft new flavors, experiences, and connections ensuring that every visit feels both familiar and fresh.
